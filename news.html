What the group has been up to.

  1. Apr 2026Preprint

    Morphological complexity of NGC 628

    A multiwavelength, multiscale analysis of NGC 628 with the ordinal pattern framework finds a characteristic scale of about 200 parsecs.

    arXiv:2604.08409 →
  2. 22 Mar 2026Event

    Astronomy on Tap Bengaluru

    Prava was featured at the Astronomy on Tap Bengaluru evening at The Green Door Coffee House, Infantry Road. See the photos in the gallery.

    Gallery →
  3. Mar 2026Preprint

    Morphological signatures in large-scale structure

    New preprint on the effects of gravitational evolution, redshift-space distortions and massive neutrinos on the morphology of the matter field, using the Quijote simulations.

    arXiv:2603.19734 →
  4. Jan 2026Preprint

    Minkowski Functionals of the 21 cm signal

    New preprint on using Minkowski Functionals of the 21 cm signal to probe primordial features from inflation.

    arXiv:2601.19450 →
  5. Nov 2025Preprint

    The CatWISE2020 quasar dipole, reassessed

    A simulation-based reassessment reduces the significance of the claimed cosmic dipole anomaly to about 3.3 to 3.6 sigma.

    arXiv:2511.00822 →
